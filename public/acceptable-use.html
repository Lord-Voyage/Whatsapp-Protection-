Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Allowed use

Use WhatsApp Protection to manage accounts you own or are authorized to administer, inspect your own connection status, configure reasonable automation limits, and receive security notices.

Prohibited use

  • Unsolicited bulk messaging, spam, scams, phishing or deceptive outreach.
  • Harassment, threats, impersonation or unlawful surveillance.
  • Malware distribution, credential theft, account takeover or session hijacking.
  • Attempting to bypass platform limits, enforcement or security controls.
  • Connecting another person's account without clear authorization.
  • Using the service in violation of applicable law or WhatsApp/Meta terms.

Rate controls

Use conservative message rates and opt-in recipient lists. The platform may apply cooldowns or suspend automation where activity appears abusive. These controls are not a guarantee against WhatsApp restrictions.

Enforcement

We may disable commands, disconnect sessions or suspend access when needed to protect users, comply with law or respond to abuse.
