Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This is a starter policy template. Before public launch, replace the operator/contact placeholders and have the policy reviewed for the jurisdictions where you operate.

1. Information we may process

WhatsApp Protection may process the phone number you submit for pairing, WhatsApp account identifiers exposed by the connected integration, messages received by the bot for command handling and SOAM activity analysis, connection status, security preferences, technical logs and support messages. SOAM security events store a short hash for repeated-text detection rather than retaining the repeated message body. Do not send passwords, SMS verification codes, payment-card data or private content that is not needed for the service.

2. How information is used

Information is used to provide pairing and command features, authenticate account owners, store security preferences, troubleshoot failures, detect abuse, maintain service reliability and respond to support requests.

3. WhatsApp session data

Where a compatible WhatsApp Web integration is enabled, its authentication state can grant access to the linked WhatsApp account. It must be stored encrypted or in an access-restricted persistent volume, never committed to source control, and never exposed in the website dashboard. Users should be able to disconnect and request deletion of their session data.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by hosting, database and infrastructure providers that are necessary to operate the service, subject to appropriate safeguards. We may disclose information when legally required or to protect users and the service.

5. Retention and deletion

Retain only what is necessary for the stated purposes. Activity logs, security events and pairing history are automatically pruned after 90 days; linked-account metadata and Baileys authentication remain until the session is disconnected or the data is otherwise removed by the operator. Users should be able to request account closure and deletion of stored session files, logs and profile data, subject to legal obligations.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no internet service can promise absolute security. Users must protect their devices, keep WhatsApp updated and never share pairing or verification codes.

7. Children

The service is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law. Do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Your choices

You may disable optional monitoring preferences, disconnect linked sessions and contact the operator to request access, correction or deletion where applicable.

Website: WhatsApp Protection
