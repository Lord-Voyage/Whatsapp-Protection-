YOUR CHAT IS THE CONTROL PANEL

Simple commands.
Clear responses.

Send a command to the WhatsApp Protection bot from your WhatsApp account. Only commands supported by the connected engine will change settings.

Read command guide ↗
/menuShow the available commands
/developersShow developer information
/activate ban_protectionLimit outbound bot replies to reduce spam-like bursts
/activate bug_protectionEnable error diagnostics and reconnect recovery
/activate soam_protectionMonitor suspicious activity and malicious command attempts
/deactivate <feature>Turn a protection off again
/statusShow which protections are on
GETTING STARTED

Three steps to
get connected.

Pair only a WhatsApp account you own or are authorized to administer. Never share your verification code or session credentials.

01

Connect

Open the pairing service and request a pairing code for your own account.

02

Confirm

Complete the linking process inside WhatsApp on your phone. The pairing service reports success or failure.

03

Manage

Open the WhatsApp Protection bot chat and use the available commands to manage settings.

PAIRING SERVICE

Connect your
WhatsApp account.

Request a real Baileys pairing code from the connected WhatsApp Protection service. The bot service must remain running and its private session storage must be persistent.

Never enter your WhatsApp six-digit SMS verification code here. Pairing codes are generated by the connected engine and entered only in WhatsApp's Linked devices flow.
Your pairing code

On your phone: WhatsApp → Linked devices → Link a device → Link with phone number.
